GD2027 explores gas discharges across pressure regimes, from thermal to non-thermal plasmas, and the technologies they make possible.

01

Gas discharges & plasmas

Gas discharges from low to high pressure, spanning thermal and non-thermal plasmas.

Gas discharges and their applications in pulsed-power technology.

03

Light sources

Gas discharges and plasma applications in light sources.

04

Switchgear

Gas discharges and their applications in switchgear.

05

Environmental technologies

Plasma and gas-discharge applications in environmental technologies.

06

Medical applications

Gas discharges and plasmas for medical applications.

These areas reflect the conference call for abstracts. Detailed programme information will be announced in due course.
