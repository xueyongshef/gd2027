Welcome to GD2027

Call for Abstracts

We are pleased to invite you to participate in the 24th International Conference on Gas Discharges and their Applications (GD2027), which will be held in Liverpool, United Kingdom, 12–17 September 2027.

The conference will be hosted by the Department of Electrical Engineering and Electronics at the University of Liverpool.

The conference will bring together researchers and practitioners working on gas discharges and their applications. Topics span low- to high-pressure plasmas and thermal to non-thermal plasmas, with applications including pulsed-power technology, light sources, switchgear, environmental technologies and medicine.

Abstract submissions

We welcome abstracts on gas discharges and their applications. Submissions should follow the conference abstract template, which will be available below once published.
