About GD2027

GD2027 will bring together researchers and practitioners at the University of Liverpool from 12–17 September 2027. The Department of Electrical Engineering and Electronics will host the conference.

The series covers gas discharges across a range of pressures and temperatures, including thermal and non-thermal plasmas. Applications include pulsed power, lighting, switchgear, environmental technologies and medicine.
